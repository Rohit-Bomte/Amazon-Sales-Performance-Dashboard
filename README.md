# 💼 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview  
This project features an interactive sales dashboard for Amazon products, developed using Microsoft Power BI. The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, enabling data-driven decision-making. It offers insights into key performance indicators (KPIs), sales trends over time, and performance by product category.  

This tool is designed for business analysts, sales managers, and stakeholders to monitor and analyze sales data, identify top-performing products, and understand customer purchasing patterns.  

---

## Key Features & Insights  
The dashboard is composed of several interactive components that provide a multi-faceted view of the sales data:

### 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)  
A set of high-level cards at the top provides an at-a-glance summary of overall performance:

- **Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales**: Total revenue generated since the beginning of the year.  
- **Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Sales**: Total revenue generated in the current quarter.  
- **YTD Products Sold**: The total number of units sold since the beginning of the year.  
- **YTD Reviews**: Total number of reviews received, indicating customer engagement.  

---

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis  
Visualizations that track sales performance over different timeframes:

- **Sales by Month**: An area chart that visualizes monthly sales trends, helping to identify seasonal patterns and peak sales periods.  
- **Sales by Week**: A bar chart that breaks down sales on a weekly basis, offering a more granular view of performance fluctuations.  

---

### 📦 Product & Category Performance  
Detailed analysis of what's selling and who the top performers are:

- **Sales by Product Category**: A detailed matrix showing YTD Sales, QTD Sales, and the percentage contribution of each category to the total YTD sales.  
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Sales**: A horizontal bar chart highlighting the most profitable products.  
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews**: A horizontal bar chart showcasing products with the highest customer engagement and feedback.  

---

### ⚙️ Interactive Controls  
- **Filters**: The dashboard includes slicers for Product Category and Quarter, allowing users to dynamically filter the entire report and drill down into specific segments for deeper analysis.  

---

## Data Source  
The insights in this report are derived from a transactional dataset of Amazon product sales. The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled within Power BI to facilitate analysis.  

---

## Tools Used  
- **Microsoft Power BI**: Used for data modeling, analysis, and visualization.  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Used for creating custom calculations and measures (e.g., YTD Sales, % YTD Sale).  

---

## How to Use  
- **Interact with the visuals**: Hover over data points on charts to see detailed tooltips.  
- **Apply Filters**: Use the slicers on the left-hand side to filter the report by Product Category or Quarter. All visuals on the page will update dynamically based on your selections.  
- **Cross-Filtering**: Click on elements within a visual (e.g., a specific category in the table) to cross-filter other visuals on the page.
